# Responsible AI Impact Assessment: [feature name]

Stage: AI overlay, active whenever the product contains a model and no financial or data regulator applies to it; feeds [Gate 3: architecture and risks reviewed](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md), and its stop condition is re-read at Gate 6 (outcomes verified)
Knowledge: [AI products](../../knowledge/domains/ai-products.md)
Skill: [AI PRD skill](../../skills/ai-prd/SKILL.md), whose must-refuse and must-escalate tables supply sections 1 and 6 here

**What checks this.** On the default path, where you fill this copy in an editor and a named human signs the gate form, Gate 3 is signed by the architect or senior engineer, the product owner and the security reviewer, per [os/STAGE-GATES.md](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md), and editing the document afterwards does not stale that signature: no hash binds it to the bytes. If you are running the optional `pmos` runtime, `python3 tools/init_product.py <product> --add` has already stamped this copy's artifact block from the frontmatter above, and approving Gate 3 with `pmos gate --bank-id design` records the copy's revision, so a later edit does stale that approval until the gate is proved again. Either way, no tool checks whether what you write is right; the people who sign the gate do. This paragraph is generated by `python3 tools/what_checks.py`.

**When this is the wrong document.**

- **A financial or data regulator applies to the product.** That is the condition [os/STAGE-GATES.md](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md) sets for the regulated overlay. Start with the [reg-gap-check skill](../../skills/reg-gap-check/SKILL.md), which routes the work to the regulated module and its stricter template, and come back here only for what that route leaves uncovered. Whether a regulator applies is a call your legal or compliance owner makes and writes down; the header below records that call, it does not make it.
- **The legal owner classifies the use as high-risk under the EU AI Act.** This file is not the documentation that classification requires. Take it to reg-gap-check and the legal owner before filling anything here.
- **The question is personal data.** Those duties are answered in the [privacy impact assessment](../architecture/privacy-impact-assessment.md), which sits beside this file; neither replaces the other.
- **The only question is whether the model is accurate enough.** That is the [eval spec](eval-spec.md).

## 3. Harms by severity and likelihood

<!-- Scales, defined once so two people score a harm the same way.
     Severity, worst credible outcome for the person harmed: 1 minor inconvenience,
     easily undone; 2 noticeable cost in time or money, undone with effort; 3 lasting
     cost, distress, or a decision about the person that is hard to reverse; 4 serious
     harm to health, livelihood or safety, or a hard-to-reverse decision repeated

## 8. How this assessment fails

<!-- Every row below is an assessment that was filled in and still protected nobody.
     Check the tells against your own draft before Gate 3. A row whose tell you find
     is not a reason to start over; it names the one section to redo. -->

| Failure | The tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Written after the decisions it describes | The date is after Gate 3, or no mitigation column names a design change | Hold it to DESIGN; an assessment that changed nothing assessed nothing |
| Only users are listed | Section 2 has no non-user row | Run the consequence scan with the question "who is affected and never opens this?" |
| Fairness read from the aggregate | Section 4 holds one number, or segments with no n | Split by segment, state n, apply the floor |
| A rate on a handful of cases | A segment below the floor is reported as a percentage | Rewrite it as "x of n" and open a field for the missing cases |
| The disclaimer is the mitigation | The mitigation column reads "terms of service" or "user is informed" | Find a design control, or score the harm as unmitigated |
| Appeal to the same decider | The decided-by column names the role, or the model, that made the first call | Route appeals to a different person with authority to reverse |
| A stop condition nobody will call | The threshold has no role, or every role owns the growth number | Add a role outside the growth line, and test the switch |
| Severity scored on the typical case | A product that touches safety, money or reputation has no severity 4 or 5 row | Rescore on the worst credible outcome |
