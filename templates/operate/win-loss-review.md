**What checks this.** A filled copy is bound to Gate 6 once it carries the artifact block, which `python3 tools/init_product.py <product> --stamp` writes from this template's own frontmatter. Approving Gate 6 with `pmos gate --bank-id operate` then records the copy's revision, and any later edit makes that approval stale until the gate is proved again. Gate 6 is signed by the product owner and the sponsor, per [os/STAGE-GATES.md](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md). No tool checks whether what you write is right; the people who sign the gate do.
