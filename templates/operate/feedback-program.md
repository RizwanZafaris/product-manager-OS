<!-- Programs are bets, and bets have kill conditions. Write them at launch,

## Exit gate (feeds Gate 6: outcomes verified)

- [ ] The charter names one recurring decision, its forum, and its cadence
- [ ] Recruiting criteria and curation rules are written, including how a seat is retired
- [ ] Incentives are non-cash and stated; NDA and data-handling terms are settled before the first session
- [ ] Every input becomes a tagged evidence note within the capture rule's deadline
- [ ] Members hear back what their input changed
- [ ] The program's own exit criteria are written and dated, and the next program review is scheduled
