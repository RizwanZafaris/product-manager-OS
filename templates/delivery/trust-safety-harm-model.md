**When this is the wrong document.**

- **No user's content or action can reach another user.** Write "N/A because [reason]" at Gate 5 and stop.
- **The attack is on the system itself** (credential stuffing, scraping, denial of service). That belongs to the threat walk in [security-architecture.md](../architecture/security-architecture.md); this file takes only its consequence for users, such as a taken-over account messaging buyers.
- **The question is what the law requires.** Notice-and-action, statements of reasons, transparency reporting, child-protection and legal reporting duties are the legal owner's call, recorded per market in the [compliance impact assessment](../operate/compliance-impact-assessment.md). This file models harm and controls; it does not say what any regime demands.
- **A model generates the content.** Its own outputs are covered by [red-team-review.md](../ai/red-team-review.md) and [guardrails.md](../ai/guardrails.md). A model used by bad actors is still a vector here.

## 7. How this model fails while looking complete

<!-- Each row is a harm model that was finished, reviewed and filed, and still did
     not protect anyone. The failure is never a missing section; it is a filled one
     that means nothing. Do not sign Gate 5 while a tell below is present and
     unexplained. -->

| Failure | The tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Every control is detection | The prevent column is empty or says "policy" | Add one design change per T1 and T2 vector before launch |
| The tail hides in an average | Time to action is a mean, or has no tier cut | Report p90 per tier, and alert on queue age, not depth alone |
| Staffed for launch day | Section 5 has no 10x column | Do the arithmetic at ten times and name what changes before you get there |
| Only users can report | No entry point for someone without an account | Add a logged-out report route for bystanders and owners of stolen or impersonated material |
| Enforcement counts as the health metric | Section 6 has action counts and no prevalence | Sample and label views; publish the interval with the estimate |
| Appeals to the same desk | The appeal decider is the original reviewer, or the same automated rule | Route appeals to a different reviewer with authority to reverse |
| Controls assumed to work | The evidence column says "assumed" and section 8 is empty | Every "assumed" is a gap with an owner and a date |
| The tier set by the typical case | A vector that can lead to violence or a stolen identity sits in T3 | Re-tier on the worst credible outcome |
