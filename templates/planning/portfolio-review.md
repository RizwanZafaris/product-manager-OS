# Portfolio Review: [portfolio or business unit], [period]

**Stage:** PLANNING track (reviewed on its own cadence rather than at a gate; its decisions reshape the portfolio-level [roadmap](roadmap.md), and a sunset it decides is recorded at that line's [Gate 6: outcomes verified](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md))
**Knowledge:** [portfolio allocation worksheet](../../frameworks/strategy/portfolio-allocation.md)
**Skill:** [roadmap-builder](../../skills/roadmap-builder/SKILL.md) for the portfolio-level roadmap these decisions reshape; [decision-memo](../../skills/decision-memo/SKILL.md) for any harvest or sunset call, which is a one-way door for the customers on that line

**What checks this.** This template declares no gate, so a filled copy joins no gate's approval manifest and editing it later stales nothing. That is deliberate: a document revised on a cadence, such as an OKR sheet or an exec update, would otherwise invalidate whichever gate it had been bound to every time it was revised. It is an input to whichever gate is open, and the people who sign that gate decide what to do with it, per [os/STAGE-GATES.md](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md). No tool checks whether what you write is right. This paragraph is generated by `python3 tools/what_checks.py`.

Filled example: [Tallowmere, Q3 2026 results and Q4 allocation](../../examples/tallowmere-portfolio-review.md)

## How this review fails

| Failure | The tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Allocation by precedent | The move column in section 4 is zero for the third review running while the gaps stay open | Either move within the limit or re-set the target in writing; a gap nobody acts on is a target nobody holds |
| Shared cost allocated to win an argument | A line's contribution changes sign when someone proposes a new allocation key | No shared-cost allocation in section 3; argue the shared cost once, at portfolio level |
| Revenue that is not revenue | The revenue column does not reconcile to finance's ledger, or says "ARR" in a footnote | Recognized revenue only, reconciled by the finance partner, or an OPEN row with an owner |
| Harvest without a floor | A harvested line's support backlog or incident count rises and nobody owns it | Every harvest names its floor in people and what that floor covers |
| Sunset by neglect | A line at negative contribution for three periods keeps the decision "sustain" | Negative contribution for two periods forces invest, harvest or sunset, with the reason written |
| H3 judged on margin | An H3 bet is killed in its first tranche for negative contribution | H3 bets are judged only against their milestone in section 5 |
| Everything is invest | The people asked for across invest rows exceed what section 4 allows | Rank the invest rows; the ones below the allocatable line become sustain this period |
| Decided outside the room | Section 6 arrives filled in the pre-read and nobody changes a cell | The pre-read carries sections 1 to 4; sections 5 and 6 are filled in the meeting |

## Exit gate

Done when every box is honestly ticked. This file declares no gate; its decisions travel to the gates of the lines they touch.

- [ ] Every line and bet drawing on the pool appears in section 1, and the people in section 4 sum to the allocatable pool
- [ ] Every row in section 2 carries its target, its source system and a cause or "unexplained"
- [ ] Section 3 uses recognized revenue reconciled with the finance partner, or the gap is an open item with an owner; no shared cost is allocated; contribution = revenue - direct cost checks on every row
- [ ] Every horizon target has a written reason, and the three gaps sum to zero
- [ ] This period's move is within the move limit, or the exception is recorded with its reason
- [ ] Every bet in section 5 has an evidence level with a dated source, a tranche within its cap, and a kill criterion written before the tranche starts
- [ ] Every line that earns revenue in section 3 has exactly one of invest, sustain, harvest or sunset, with an owner and a reversal condition
- [ ] Every invest above the E2 cap names its business case, every harvest names its floor, and every sunset names its metrics review and its sunset plan
- [ ] Dissent is recorded with names, and every decision is in the [decision log](../execution/decision-log.md)
- [ ] The portfolio-level [roadmap](roadmap.md) and the [capacity plan](capacity-plan.md) have been updated to match section 6, or the date they will be is written here
