**Skill:** [decision-memo](../../skills/decision-memo/SKILL.md) for each integrate, keep separate or sunset call; [program-premortem](../../skills/program-premortem/SKILL.md) for the integration risk pass

**What checks this.** This template declares no gate, so a filled copy joins no gate's approval manifest and editing it later stales nothing. That is deliberate: a document revised on a cadence, such as an OKR sheet or an exec update, would otherwise invalidate whichever gate it had been bound to every time it was revised. It is an input to whichever gate is open, and the people who sign that gate decide what to do with it, per [os/STAGE-GATES.md](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md). No tool checks whether what you write is right. This paragraph is generated by `python3 tools/what_checks.py`.

## 9. How this plan fails

| Failure | The tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The thesis stays in the deck | Six months after close nobody can say which synergy is on track, because none had a threshold | Section 1 rewritten as hypotheses by day 30, each with a kill signal and a named caller |
| Integrate everything by default | Every acquired line has the same decision, made in week one, with no hypothesis cited | One decision per line in section 3, each tied to a hypothesis, and keep separate written down as a real option |
| Sunset by rumour | Acquired customers ask support whether their product is being shut down before anyone has decided | One dated announcement per sunset line, through the sunset plan, and a holding statement for the lines still undecided |
| Commitments found at renewal | A customer produces an order-form promise the acquirer never knew it had made | Section 4 built from contracts by day 30, with legal reading the order forms, not the CRM |
| Integration eats the core roadmap | Our own product's delivery slows for two quarters and nobody connects it to the integration | The capacity guardrail in section 8, read monthly beside the [capacity plan](capacity-plan.md) |
| Key people leave at the vest date | The engineer who understood the matching logic leaves on the day the bonus pays | Knowledge transfer to a named second person before any retention payment vests |
| Synergy counted twice | Cross-sell revenue appears in both business units' plans | One owner per hypothesis metric, and the finance partner reconciles the two plans each quarter |
