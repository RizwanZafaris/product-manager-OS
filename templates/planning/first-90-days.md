     a manager's output is the team's output, so nothing here counts unless it
     changes what the team produces; and delegation is calibrated to task-relevant
     maturity, which you cannot judge before day 30 and must judge by day 60.

## 3. Days 1 to 30: learn, and change nothing structural

**Posture:** listen, document, and earn the right to be wrong later.

| # | Action | Output that proves it happened | By day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet every function that can block a launch: engineering, design, data, support, sales, legal, finance, operations | First pass of [stakeholder map](../execution/stakeholder-map.md), with concerns in their words | 15 |
| 2 | Read the last two quarters of decisions and postmortems | A list of the three decisions you would have made differently, kept private for now | 20 |
| 3 | Watch the product being used by a real customer, unassisted | Notes in a research file, plus one thing that surprised you | 25 |
| 4 | Find the numbers: what is instrumented, what is trusted, what is quoted and unsourced | A one-page metric inventory naming each metric's owner and source system | 30 |

## 4. Days 31 to 60: form a view and test it in public

**Posture:** commit to a reading, invite the strongest objection to it.

| # | Action | Output | By day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Write your diagnosis of the product's central problem, in one page | A document your team can argue with, circulated before it is presented | 40 |
| 2 | Take the diagnosis to the three people most likely to disagree | Their objections written into the document, with your answer or your concession | 45 |
| 3 | Decide which loop stage the product is actually in and what gate it skipped | An entry in [decision-log.md](../execution/decision-log.md) naming the gap | 50 |
| 4 | Rework the roadmap with the team, not for them | A draft in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) with something visibly killed | 60 |
| 5 | Judge task-relevant maturity per person for the work you intend to delegate | Your own private note, revisited quarterly | 60 |
