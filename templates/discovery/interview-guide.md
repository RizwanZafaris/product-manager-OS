## 4. Question blocks

<!-- Ask the question, then probe with "what happened next", "what did that
     cost you", "show me". Time budgets keep the last block from being cut every
     time. Questions in the customer's vocabulary, never in the product's. -->

### Block A: context (RQ: [ids], [n] minutes)

| Question | Probes | Listening for |
|---|---|---|
| Tell me about your role and where [target activity] fits in a normal week. | How often? Who else is involved? | frequency, actors, tools |

### Block B: the last time (RQ: [ids], [n] minutes)

| Question | Probes | Listening for |
|---|---|---|
| Walk me through the last time you [target activity], from what triggered it to when it was done. | What did you do right before? What happened after? Show me, if you can. | the real sequence, not the ideal one |
| Where did that go slower or worse than you wanted? | What did you do about it? What did that cost you: time, money, standing? | pain with a cost attached |

### Block C: workarounds and tools (RQ: [ids], [n] minutes)

| Question | Probes | Listening for |
|---|---|---|
| What have you tried to fix or avoid this? | Did you pay for anything? Build anything? Who set it up? | spend, effort, ownership |
| What did you stop using, and why? | What was the moment you gave up? | switching evidence |

### Block D: forces (RQ: [ids], [n] minutes)

<!-- What pushes them away from today, what pulls them toward something else,
     what makes them anxious about switching, what habit holds them in place.
     Listen for all four; anxiety and habit are the ones teams forget to ask
     about. -->

| Question | Probes | Listening for |
|---|---|---|
| When you last considered changing how you do this, what got in the way? | What would have had to be true? Who else had a say? | anxiety, habit |
| What would make it worth the hassle of changing? | Has anything come close? | pull |

### Block E: priority and commitment (RQ: [ids], [n] minutes)

| Question | Probes | Listening for |
|---|---|---|
| Of everything we discussed, what would you fix first, and why that one? | What would you do with the time or money back? | ranked pain |
| If a fix for [the pain they named] existed, what would you do next: try a rough version, introduce a colleague, put time on a calendar? | When? | commitment, not compliments |

## 5. Question rules

| Never ask | Because | Ask instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Would you use a product that..." | hypothetical enthusiasm is free | "When did you last look for something like that? What happened?" |
| "Do you think this is a problem?" | it leads the witness | "When did this last cost you something? What?" |
| "How much would you pay?" | nobody knows in the abstract | "What do you spend on this today, including time?" |
| Anything that explains the idea before Block E | the session stops producing evidence | park it for the close |

## 6. Close (3 minutes)

- Thank them; ask who else you should talk to, and for an introduction now if one is offered
- Ask for the artifacts they mentioned: [the spreadsheet, the email chain, the screenshot]
- Only now, if asked, describe the idea in one sentence, and record the reaction as a compliment, not evidence
- Confirm follow-up: [what they agreed to, by when; goes to interview-notes.md section 5]
