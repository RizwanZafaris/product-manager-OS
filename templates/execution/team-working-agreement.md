# Team Working Agreement: [team name], [period]

**Stage:** ALL STAGES (agreed when the trio forms, usually in DISCOVER, and re-read at every gate in [STAGE-GATES](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md); it declares no gate of its own)
**Knowledge:** [triad decision rights](../../knowledge/roles/triad-decision-rights.md); [Grove on managerial output](../../knowledge/high-output-management.md), which names remote-first communication as outside what its frame covers; this file rebuilds its meeting taxonomy for asynchronous work
**Skill:** [escalation](../../skills/escalation/SKILL.md) for a disagreement that leaves the trio; [product-review](../../skills/product-review/SKILL.md) for the weekly review ritual

**What checks this.** This template declares no gate, so a filled copy joins no gate's approval manifest and editing it later stales nothing. That is deliberate: a document revised on a cadence, such as an OKR sheet or an exec update, would otherwise invalidate whichever gate it had been bound to every time it was revised. It is an input to whichever gate is open, and the people who sign that gate decide what to do with it, per [os/STAGE-GATES.md](../../os/STAGE-GATES.md). No tool checks whether what you write is right. This paragraph is generated by `python3 tools/what_checks.py`.

## 3. Async-first defaults

<!-- Async-first means writing is the default medium and a meeting is the

- **Written decisions.** A decision made in a call does not exist until it is in the [decision log](decision-log.md), written by its first voice within [one working day]. Anyone who was absent may reopen it within [two working days] with new evidence; after that it stands until its reopen trigger fires.
- **Proposals.** A change that needs more than one seat starts as a written proposal with a decide-by date. Silence by that date means [consent / no decision]: say which, never leave it implied.
- **Handoffs across time zones.** Whoever's day ends first writes [three lines: what moved, what is blocked, what the next person needs] where the next person starts their day.
- **Meeting-free blocks.** [days or hours, in each person's local time]. Exceptions: [production incidents only, or name them].
- **Missed meetings.** A meeting someone could not attend is [recorded and summarized / summarized only] in writing by [who], within [n working hours].

| Channel | Use it for | Expected first response | Not for |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5. How disagreement escalates

<!-- The dispute path from the triad decision rights card, with this team's time
     limits written in, then the escalation skill's ladder. Escalate the

1. **Restate it as a falsifiable disagreement**, in writing, within [n working days] of it surfacing: which of the four risks is contested, and what evidence would settle it.
2. **Buy the evidence at the smallest price**, timeboxed to [n working days].
3. **If the evidence cannot arrive in time, the first voice decides.** The decision log records the call, the dissent and the trigger that reopens it.
4. **Still stuck, or outside the trio's rights:** a six-part brief per the [escalation skill](../../skills/escalation/SKILL.md), sent to rung 2: [the managers, named], who answer within [3 business days].

## 6. Review

Review on [YYYY-MM-DD], and early when any of these happens: a seat changes hands; someone joins from a time zone that changes section 1's overlap; a sync ritual is skipped three times running; a dispute reaches step 4; a response-time expectation is missed often enough that someone has stopped relying on it.

## How this agreement fails

| Failure | The tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Written once, never read | Nobody can say the expected response time for document comments without opening the file | Read section 3 aloud at the first retrospective after each review date, and change whatever nobody follows |
| Async in name, sync in practice | Decisions keep happening in calls, and the decision log trails them by days | A decision not logged within the agreed time is reopened; the first voice writes it or loses it |
| The overlap belongs to one person | The same seat always works the early or late hours that make the window exist | Rotate who flexes, or write down who flexes, why, and what they get in return |
| Status in the overlap window | The scarcest live hours are spent reading progress aloud | Status is written; the window is for decisions and disputes |
| Rights that are moods | A first voice overrules without evidence, and the other seats stop raising risks | Every override carries its evidence in writing, per the partner rule in the triad card |
| Escalation as ambush | A manager hears about a dispute before the counterpart has seen the brief | The counterpart sees the brief first, every time |
| Silence read both ways | One person read a missed decide-by date as consent, another as no decision | Section 3 states which one silence means |
