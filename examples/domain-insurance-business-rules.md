## 4. Change control

- **Who may change a rule:** the business owner of the rule, not the product team. Claims rules (BR-INS-04 to BR-INS-09) are owned by the Chief Claims Officer, Priya Venkataraman. Underwriting eligibility rules (BR-INS-01 to BR-INS-03) are owned by the Chief Underwriting Officer.
- **How a change lands:** for any claims threshold change, actuarial and compliance sign-off are both required before the change is made. A claims-handling threshold (such as BR-INS-04's fast-track dollar value or BR-INS-06's total-loss percentage) is a conduct and process rule, not a rating variable, so it does not by itself require a new rate filing; actuarial reviews it for its effect on loss-cost assumptions used in the next rate filing, but the change itself lands as a claims-manual update. A filed and approved rate change is required only where a rule change directly alters a premium, rating factor, or rating tier, which is why BR-INS-01 through BR-INS-03 (underwriting eligibility, which sets the rate applied) carry the rate-filing requirement below and the claims threshold rows do not. For any underwriting eligibility change, the change is versioned with the rate-filing reference under BR-INS-03 and reaches production only after the filing is approved or deemed effective. The request route is: rule owner raises the change, actuarial and compliance review it, the filing record is updated, then the release path carries the change to enforced behaviour. A rule change never rides a release that has already passed Gate 2 without re-review.
- **Review cadence:** the register is re-confirmed against its sources quarterly. The state-by-state limits in BR-INS-07 are confirmed against the governing act before each state go-live, because the NAIC model is adopted with variation and the numbers differ by state.

### Worked micro-example (illustrative, invented)

> **BR-INS-04:** WHEN a first-notice-of-loss claim's estimated value is below USD 2,500 AND the policy is within its first 90 days of no prior claims THEN route to fast-track adjudication, else route to a licensed human adjuster. Trigger: claims intake, on FNOL submission. Source of truth: Claims-handling manual v4, held by the Chief Claims Officer. Business owner: Chief Claims Officer. Exceptions: none on the USD 2,500 threshold; a policy with a prior denial in the last 12 months is excluded regardless of value. Enforced by FR-CLM-12. Test: AC-CLM-03.
> In June 2026 the threshold moved from USD 2,000 to USD 2,500 and the first-90-days condition was added. The change entered through section 4, BR-INS-00 was retired into section 2, and BR-INS-04 replaced it, refiled with the state Department of Insurance. The history explains why a May claim and a July claim with the same estimated value behaved differently.
