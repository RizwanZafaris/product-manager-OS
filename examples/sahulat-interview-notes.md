## 7. Interpretation

- Strongest moment of the session: 02:10, where one missed Sunday due date produced three separate costs at once, a shut bank, a fifty-rupee shop fee, and a two-hundred-rupee fine carried onto the next bill.
- What it might mean for the research questions, marked as interpretation, not fact:
  - RQ1: a due date landing on a weekend looks like it turns a free trip into a paid one and adds a fine on top. This is the sector card's point that an informal cash workaround is not a free one ([mobile money and wallets](../knowledge/domains/mobile-money-wallets.md), question 2); the trip, the shop fee and the fine are exactly that cost, priced. Worth checking against the other sessions with a Saturday or Sunday due date before treating it as a pattern rather than one household's bad month.
  - RQ2: a wallet balance that empties the same day it is funded, twice by her own account in this session, is one data point against a pay-from-balance design. INT-003 and INT-006 still stand on the other side of it, so this reads as an open question the personas will have to carry, not a verdict this session can settle on its own.
  - RQ4: a dropped session produced a repeat attempt within minutes, not a wait for a status message, consistent with the fuller account INT-007 gave of the same kind of moment.
- Contradicts or confirms earlier sessions: confirms the trip-and-fee pattern from INT-001, INT-002, INT-005 and INT-008, and the weekend-surcharge pattern from INT-005 and INT-008. On whether customers hold a wallet balance, this session sits against INT-003 and INT-006, where the participant described keeping a salary balance for a different reason; the split is not resolved by one more session on either side.
- What we still could not learn from this participant: whether she would actually leave money sitting in the wallet once a bill-pay flow exists, since the interviewer's description of the idea came late (38:05) and the two lines after it, including her "I would probably try it," are weaker for that reason. Also did not learn how she would react to a wallet-only payment record in place of the paper bills she keeps in a folder; that question was not on the v2 guide and was not improvised.
