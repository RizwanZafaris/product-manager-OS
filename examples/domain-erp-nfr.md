## 6. Data retention

| Data class | Retention period | Deletion behavior | Driven by (policy, regulation, choice) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General Ledger Entries | 7 years | Archive to cold storage; immutable read-only access | Statutory requirement (SOX/GAAP) | Compliance Officer, Elena Ross |
| Audit Logs | 7 years | Archive to cold storage; searchable | SOX ITGC evidence | Compliance Officer, Elena Ross |
| Vendor Master Data | Indefinite until contract end + 7 years | Soft delete; hidden from search but retained for audit | Business policy | Product Manager, Elias Thorne |

*"Searchable" for Audit Logs means the 30-second, one-month-range query NFR in section 1 applies to the current and prior rolling month, which stays in hot storage; a query reaching back into the cold-storage years is not bound by that 30-second target, and no target is set for it here. This distinction is a gap, not an oversight papered over: the external auditor's actual query pattern at year-end walkthrough will confirm whether a cold-storage query target is needed.*
