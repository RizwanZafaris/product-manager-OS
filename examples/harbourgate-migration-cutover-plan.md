## 4. Rollback

- Rollback trigger, agreed in advance: cohort decline rate above baseline + 2 percentage points over 30 minutes, or Kestrel 5xx responses above 2% of calls over 5 minutes; called by on-call without discussion (N17)
- Decision owner during the window: on-call engineer calls the trigger; Tomasz Wierzbicki, cutover lead, has abort authority for any step that is not a clear trigger · Reachable via: on-call channel
- Point of no return: removal of a provider's routing flag, 14 days after that provider reaches 100% (N49): Marlowe 2026-08-03, Tidewater 2026-08-31, Kestrel legacy connector 2026-09-21 · Announced how, when reached: on-call channel, with the flag-removal event itself logged under BR-007
- Rollback steps before the point of no return: flip the per-provider flag back to the previous cohort or to legacy, two-person change (BR-007), owner Tomasz Wierzbicki · rehearsed 2026-07-04, 3 min 50 s (N40)
- Rollback for store cohorts specifically: a per-shop flag back to legacy inside the 06:00 to 07:30 window (N48), owner Tomasz Wierzbicki, with Lena Baptiste informed. This is the mechanism R6 (a kiosk cohort flip failing while shops are trading) is mitigated by, distinct from the per-provider flag flip above
- Abort during a pre-production rehearsal: every pre-production schedule is disabled first (A5, added 2026-06-19), verified at the rehearsal 2 abort drill on 2026-06-27; this is what stops the HG-INC-14 failure mode, a scheduled job consuming a live file, from recurring
- Restore steps after the point of no return: re-enable the removed flag and restore legacy routing, owner Tomasz Wierzbicki · rehearsed 2026-07-04, 22 minutes (N40). After 2026-09-14, when termination notices went to Marlowe and Tidewater for 2026-12-15 (D-023), a restore for either provider still works technically but depends on a contract under notice; that risk is R10 and is reviewed again on 2026-12-05, not resolved by this plan. After 2026-12-15, when the Marlowe and Tidewater contracts end, restore for either provider stops being possible at all rather than merely dependent on a contract under notice; legacy credentials and wrapper logs are deleted 2027-01-15 (journey timeline; N58 for the logs), the final hard stop behind that. Flag removal at the point of no return is the last cheap point of restore, not the only limit; 2026-12-15 is where restore becomes impossible
- Refunds on legacy-authorised orders keep routing under BR-002 regardless of the routing flag's state, whether during a rollback or during the ordinary drain (R7); neither the rollback steps nor the restore steps above change refund routing
- Data written to the new system after the switch, if we roll back: kept, not replayed. Orders Quay authorised before a rollback stay on Quay's ledger and reconcile through ADR-0004's straddle set; a rollback stops new orders reaching Quay and does not touch orders already authorised

## 5. Data reconciliation

Tolerance and the daily reconciliation policy are set in Finance close policy v3 (N35): £50 per provider per day on settled versus captured value, and zero lines on count once the straddle set is classified. The classified straddle set is ADR-0004 (corrective action A1), accepted 2026-06-19; its straddle-set logic was exercised in rehearsal 2 before merge; built 2026-07-03 (AC-8 amended the same day); the amended AC-8 was verified by rehearsal 3, 2026-07-04 (N39). A failed check blocks finance close (BR-003) and stops the next cohort step's advance. The method below is a business total plus a count of unmatched lines rather than a separate sample check: a line that fails to match on reference surfaces as a non-zero unmatched-line count, but a line that matches on reference while carrying a mangled amount does not; that case surfaces only through the daily £50 business total, and only once the mismatch exceeds tolerance for the day. No line-level sample of n lines is run.
