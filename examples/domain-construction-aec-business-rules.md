### Worked micro-example (illustrative, invented)

> **BR-TW-03:** WHEN a payment run includes a subcontractor pay application THEN release only if a conditional progress waiver for this period and an unconditional progress waiver for the prior payment are on file, in the statutory form where the project state mandates one. Trigger: payment run approval. Source of truth: GC project controller's waiver register; subcontract payment clause; California Civil Code sections 8132 and 8134. Business owner: Marisol Vang. Exceptions: none. Enforced by FR-109. Test: AC-12.
> This is the slice that decides whether the whole payment workflow is worth anything. The rule is not "some paperwork is attached," it is which two specific waiver states must both be true, in the form the jurisdiction actually prescribes, or the payment can undermine the very lien protection it was supposed to secure.
>
> Worked arithmetic on Willowmere Harbor, all ILLUSTRATIVE: the electrical subcontract's schedule of values totals $1,400,000 across 20 lines. Line 12 (switchgear, stored value) is billed at $182,000 for period 6. Retainage at the subcontract's 10 percent is $18,200, so the net due on that line is $182,000 minus $18,200 = $163,800. The subcontract carries a reduction provision, and this period is the one where the subcontract crosses 50 percent completion, so retainage for this period is withheld at 5 percent instead: $182,000 x 5% = $9,100, and the release posts as a separate line of $18,200 minus $9,100 = $9,100, not as an edit to the withheld total. The payment run for period 6 clears only when a conditional progress waiver for period 6 and an unconditional progress waiver for period 5 are both on file: period 5's unconditional waiver covers $241,000 already paid (period 5's net due after its own retainage, carried forward from that period's pay application), and if the state is California both waivers must be on the statutory forms under Civil Code sections 8132 and 8134 (as of 2026-09-11, confirm with counsel). A free-text waiver does not satisfy BR-005 and the run holds.
>
> A month later the state's prompt-payment window changes: the change enters through section 4, BR-006 is retired into section 2 with its clock arithmetic, and the replacement carries the new window. The history explains why June payments and September payments ran to different clocks.
