## 2. Positioning against the named alternative

Carried unchanged from the signed positioning document (positioning, 2026-10-30), so this plan invents nothing new.

- **For** the six design-partner accounts drawn from S-5, Business-plan accounts with first-submission approval under 75% and five or more filers (N36)
- **who** bounce more than a quarter of expense reports back to the filer, the pattern six of six design-partner interviews named (paraphrased, no verbatim quote on file, EV-C01 to EV-C06, N40); median first-submission approval sits at 66% across S-1 (N35) and at 64% on the design partners' own Q3 baseline (N44)
- **Expense Copilot add-on is an** add-on to the finance platform the account already runs, not a stand-alone receipt-capture product and not a general AI assistant
- **that** drafts the line item, with matched receipt and category against the account's own policy, inside the same Expenses workflow the account already approves reports through
- **unlike** typing receipt data into the Expenses form with no draft, a shared spreadsheet kept alongside it (four of six interviewed design partners run one, N40), or buying Cinderwick, the seat-priced receipt-capture vendor two of six interviewed partners had already trialled (N13, N40)
- **ours** activates on the Business plan the account already runs and bills on that same account, no new vendor contract and no separate security review to open, for Business accounts; verifiable by watching one demo of an account admin turning it on from Expenses settings

That last claim holds for Business accounts only. R4, the customer DPA not naming the model vendor as a subprocessor, blocked two Enterprise (S-6) quotes from 2026-11-23 until D5 closed it on 2026-12-03. Enterprise buyers did have a security review to open.

Ledgerline's own rejected GTM-level alternative, added at the 2026-12-21 update rather than recorded at signing: running phase 2 as a widened S-5 phase, rather than jumping to all of S-1, was considered and not chosen. N55's sizing needs about 2,540 accounts across both arms, which S-5's 1,240 accounts cannot supply, so phase 2 went to S-1 instead; the section 3 note above shows the seat-count objection that widening exposed (WL-01 Redfern, N76). Alongside it, bundling the copilot free into the Business plan was argued twice, in the positioning session and again at the pricing review, and lost on margin both times (D2). A free rollout to all 2,900 S-1 accounts at the business case's own 60% adoption assumption would run about $212,000 a year in model cost with no revenue line, about 1.9 points of Business-plan gross margin (N51), against Daniel Okafor's rule that no feature costing more than one point of gross margin ships without its own revenue line (N50). This plan's phase 1 and phase 2 gate on a paid or discounted-paid cohort for exactly that reason; a free phase 1 would have told the team nothing about whether S-1 would pay, which is the question EXP-1 existed to answer.
