# Harbourgate Coverage Sheet: the data behind the examples outside the journey

Fills no template. This file extends [harbourgate-journey.md](harbourgate-journey.md) and never contradicts it. It is the supplementary data sheet for 36 coverage files. Thirty-one of them sit inside the Harbourgate story, and each takes every number from the journey's data sheet (N1 to N72 and its identifier tables) or from the HC rows below. The other five stand outside the story, because it cannot host them honestly, and they take nothing from here. Everything is invented: Harbourgate, Quay, Kestrel, Marlowe and Tidewater are fictional, every person is the journey's fictional cast, and every number, date, rate and pound is ILLUSTRATIVE. It was chosen so that the coverage files reconcile with the sixteen journey artifacts and with the seed. It is never to be quoted as a benchmark or copied as a target. See the [examples index](README.md).

## Rules for every coverage file

- **The journey wins.** Where this sheet and [harbourgate-journey.md](harbourgate-journey.md) could be read two ways, the journey's reading holds. No coverage file moves a journey date, owner, outcome, score or gate attempt, and no N row is restated here with a different value.
- **Two sources of numbers, nothing else.** A coverage file cites journey rows by their own ids (N17, R9, DEP-2) and this sheet's rows by HC id. Firmness uses the journey's four words: measured, estimate, target, assumption.
- **The prefix is HC.** HC is unused anywhere in the journey, its sixteen artifacts or the seed. The other new identifiers are listed in the identifier tables below, and each was checked against the journey's identifier table before it was claimed.
- **One unit for effort.** A team-day means one engineer-day in every coverage file, unless the row says finance-analyst days.
- **Decision numbers.** D-010 is the only decision-log number this sheet claims. D-001 to D-009 and D-012 to D-016 stay unassigned, and no coverage file may use them.

## Cast additions

None. Every coverage file uses the thirteen named people and the one counterparty in the journey's cast table. Where a file needs someone the cast does not name, it uses a role, as the journey does: the squad's other three engineers, a finance analyst, a fraud analyst, store associates, the external assessor.

## Reconciliation notes

These are the places where a careless draft would contradict the journey, with the reading every coverage file follows.

1. **R7's first score.** The journey's identifier table gives R7 a score of 9 "raised 2026-06-16". The risk register and the cutover plan say the premortem of 2026-04-09 seeded it. Coverage files read both as true: the story was told at the premortem, and its first register score is 9, from 2026-06-16. No coverage file states an earlier L x I for R7.
2. **R8 and R13 came from findings, not from the premortem.** They entered the register on the premortem's date from F1 and F3. The premortem worksheet's causes are R1 to R7 and R10 to R12.
3. **Scales stay with their instruments.** Register rows are scored L x I on 1 to 3 scales (N68), and that one scoring is shared with the STRIDE checklist on purpose. The premortem's votes (HC36) and the FMEA's severity, occurrence and detection (HC39) are different instruments. They are never restated as register scores. The risk-matrix worksheet's 1 to 5 scales are not run on Harbourgate rows, which is why that coverage file stands outside the story.
4. **Definition weight.** The seed chose a full PRD, and the journey's DEFINE set is that PRD, the NFR and the business rules. The business rules register's "Enforced by (FR ID)" column cites HARBOURGATE-S story ids, which is only honest because no FR-numbered FRD exists. The BRD and the FRD therefore stand outside the story.
5. **No privacy impact assessment.** Section 4 of the compliance impact assessment recorded "DPIA required: no", signed by Anneliese Vogt on 2026-07-06. The privacy impact assessment template defers the "whether" to that section, so a Harbourgate one would contradict it.
6. **No program charter.** Quay is one initiative carried by one team (N63). The charter template itself says that shape needs a PRD, not a charter, and the stakeholder map already owns the RACI a charter would hold.
7. **"No visible change" for shoppers.** The release readiness document and Ines Castellanos's Gate 5 line say shoppers saw no visible change. Coverage files describe the payment step's screens as existing components whose content or provider changed, never as a new surface, which matches NFR section 5.
8. **Two stakeholder instruments.** The power-interest grid scores of 2026-06-25 (HC42) come from behaviour in the prior 30 days. The stakeholder map's H/M/L ratings date from 2026-10-14. Lena Baptiste was the only person in the high-power, low-interest quadrant at Gate 2 (2026-04-07) and in the 2026-06-25 grid, but her interest moved to M by the 2026-10-14 stakeholder-map review, once her cadence settled into a standing weekly report by the third store cohort (2026-08-17).
