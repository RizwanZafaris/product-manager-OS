## 5. Handoff to engineering

- [x] No detail omitted: every state declared in this scope has its own spec. The states walked in the redesign are: default, guest entered (no account prompt), payment in progress, payment declined (with one-retry offer, BR-001 and AC-5), step-up challenge invoked (BR-005, N57), authorise timeout 503 (Kestrel one same-key retry, N61, FS-2 HC2: 200 injected timeouts, all returned 503 after exactly one retry), webhook suppressed (FS-5 HC3: all 50 resolved by poll fallback 30 to 34 s), confirmation. The kiosk fallback (BR-004: "pay at the till" within 10 s, basket held 30 min, N31) is a spec of its own and was walked separately
- [x] No edge case ignored: the design was checked against inputs the happy path never shows. The cognitive walkthrough and the FMEA (HC39) surfaced: a card number longer than the field can display (the Kestrel hosted fields render in an iframe, the display is the vendor's), a name longer than the address block (the address block clips with a "..." ellipsis; the existing checkout-pay behaviour, unchanged), a zero-value basket (the basket minimum applies, unchanged), a missing delivery address (the form requires it, unchanged), and a declined card where the guest has no other payment method (the decline message offers "pay at the till" for kiosks and "try another card" for web; BR-001, AC-5)
- [x] No technical limit disregarded: the design was checked against what the platform actually renders. N23: authorise latency p95 target 2,000 ms, measured 1,350 ms in the Kestrel sandbox (2026-06-05). Checkout client timeout HC5: web and app wait 20 s, above Quay's worst case of 18 s (8 s timeout, 2 s wait, 8 s retry, N61); the kiosk keeps its own 10 s fallback (BR-004). The Kestrel hosted fields render in an iframe, so the visual state of the payment step is constrained by what the iframe can display at the viewport width: F14's fix (guest entry above the fold) was verified at 320 CSS px in the accessibility walk, and the iframe's own min-width was confirmed at Kestrel's side at signing (ILLUSTRATIVE; no iframe min-width spec is on the journey or coverage sheet)

## 6. Gate 3 decision

**Decision:** APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS, because: The redesign passes Q1 and Q2 but not Q3, which is answered only by routing F2 to the usability test plan. The account-prompt removal (Q1) introduces no regression found beyond that recorded as F1; the payment step's hosted fields (Q2) are a vendor surface, accepted by ADR-0003, and the accessibility posture (HC12: WCAG 2.2 AA) was walked against four existing payment-step components with 0 findings introduced by Quay (HC13) and against three sampled surfaces on the redesign prototype with 0 new violations (HD7, HD11); the one-retry finding (F2) is severity 3 and is routed to usability test per HD6. Two severity 3 findings remain open for inspection-only reasons: F1 and F2 (HD4, counts 1 of 3 each, routed to the next moderated usability round and, in parallel, tracked as a design backlog and design-debt item outside the risk register, under Ines Castellanos). The accessibility finding AX-1 is carried to the backlog as a pre-existing finding, not closed at this gate.
