## 1. Launch facts, written once

- What is shipping, in one sentence a customer would recognize: pay your Ravi Power electricity bill or Chenab Gas bill from your Sahulat wallet, looked up by the reference number on the paper bill, over USSD or the app, with a payment reference SMS the moment it posts.
- When: 2026-07-06 · Rollout shape: staged, not all at once. First to the 600 pilot agents in the Lahore district (N6); nationwide planned for 2026-07-20, after the Gate 5 condition closes.
- Who gets it first: the 600 Lahore pilot agents (N6) and the customers who cash in through them. Open: Zainab Qureshi owns which wallets see the USSD bill-pay menu item from 2026-07-06 (a cohort gate by agent, by region, or by app version), a build-time decision this plan does not re-specify; the pilot customer SMS in section 3 should be scoped to that same gate once it is confirmed, not to the full active base.
- What changes for existing users: a bill-pay option appears in the USSD menu and the app; nothing else in the wallet changes, and the customer fee is PKR 0 per bill (D4, N33).
- What does NOT change: cash-in, cash-out and transfer are untouched; agent commission on cash-in and cash-out stays at PKR 15 and PKR 20 (N29, N30). Mehran Water bills are not available at launch; D5 dropped that scope on 2026-05-21 after three of ten test bills failed reference validation, and support scripts in section 4 say so rather than let a customer discover it mid-call.

Staged over nationwide-on-day-one was decided, not defaulted to. D6, 2026-07-01: Faisal Mirza chose a 600-agent Lahore pilot ahead of a national cutover, against two rejected options: launching to all active agents on day one, and holding the whole launch until the agent float top-up hotline, the open Gate 5 condition owned by Tariq Sohail, was live everywhere. Day-one nationwide would have run premortem risk R1, agents running out of float on a bill-peak day, at national scale before the hotline existed to answer it; holding the whole launch traded a bounded pilot risk for an open-ended delay against the board's 520,000-wallet key result (N4). The cost of staging is two cutovers instead of one, and about two weeks of nationwide key-result movement, 2026-07-06 to the planned 2026-07-20 (D6); the gap it opens below is who hears what, and when, across two rollout steps rather than one.
