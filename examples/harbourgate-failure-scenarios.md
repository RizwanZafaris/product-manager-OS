## 2. Monitoring gaps found while writing this

## 3. Rehearsal

- Scenarios rehearsed (game day or tabletop), with dates: FS-2 and FS-5 were exercised in the Kestrel sandbox on 2026-06-08, covering 200 injected timeouts and 50 suppressed status webhooks (HC2, HC3). FS-7 was exercised by the synthetic failure check on 2026-07-02, with the page firing at 07:46 and the runbook completed in 11 minutes (N72). The FS-6 rollback path was rehearsed on 2026-07-04, with the flag flip to legacy timed at 3 min 50 s and restoration after flag removal at 22 minutes (N40).
- The scenario we most doubt our recovery steps for: **FS-1 after sunset**, because its recovery depends on Kestrel region failover, targeted at 60 minutes, and Harbourgate has never rehearsed that path. The Q4 capacity plan carries a Kestrel region failover rehearsal (N29, HC33).
