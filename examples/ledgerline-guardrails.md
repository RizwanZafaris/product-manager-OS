# Guardrails: Expense Copilot drafting model, customer add-on

Fills [templates/ai/guardrails.md](../templates/ai/guardrails.md). Everything here is invented: Ledgerline is a fictional mid-market software company, the Expense Copilot add-on is the fictional product used across this repository, and every count, dollar figure, date, threshold and identifier is ILLUSTRATIVE, carried from the [journey data sheet](ledgerline-journey.md) and its supplement, the [coverage sheet](ledgerline-coverage-sheet.md), so it can be checked against the documents that cite the same rails. See the [examples index](README.md).

**Feature:** The copilot drafts an expense report for a customer account: it extracts merchant, date, amount and currency from a receipt image or forwarded email, suggests a category matched to that account's own policy line, assembles a draft the filer edits and submits, and flags low-confidence fields (under 0.80 model confidence) to the reviewer; nothing is submitted, activated, billed or corrected in policy without a human acting first.

## 2. Output constraints

Checks run on each drafted report after the model returns, before the filer ever sees a draft.

| Constraint | Trigger | Behavior | Enforcement point | Test | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Schema validation | Draft output fails the line-item schema (merchant, date, amount, currency, suggested category) | Retry once, then fail closed: the filer gets a blank, flagged draft and types the fields themselves; no malformed draft is stored | Code, post-model, in draft assembly | Malformed-output fixture in the testing strategy's edge-case register | Priya Nair (P2) |
| Claim gating: never invent | A field cannot be read off the receipt | The field is left blank and flagged, never guessed (PRD functional scope 2, following [templates/ai/hallucination-controls.md](../templates/ai/hallucination-controls.md)); a number absent from the source is stripped or abstained | Verifier step, post-model | Field-level evals: EVAL-1 (1,200 English-language internal receipts, 94% field accuracy, 88% category match, LC18); EVAL-2 (360 sanitized customer receipts, 60 per design partner, 92% and 86%, re-scored with v5 on 2026-11-06, LC19); thresholds at least 90% field accuracy and at least 85% category match (LC21) | Priya Nair (P2) |
| Confidence flagging | An extracted field or suggested category scores under 0.80 model confidence (LC22) | Flagged visually distinct to the reviewer (LEDGERLINE-S3); the flag is mandatory display, not optional UI polish | Code, post-model, in the reviewer view | AG-02 (reviewer confirms flagged fields) in the human approval gates doc | Priya Nair (P2) |

## 3. Never-do list: blocked behaviors

| Blocked behavior | Enforcement point (prompt / classifier / code / human gate) | Backstop if the first layer fails | Test | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Submit any report, ever | Code: no submit tool exists in the agent roster (least-access review of 2026-11-04, next review 2027-02-04, LC30); the PRD lists auto-submission as out of scope, a load-bearing guardrail, not a v2 candidate | Human gate: the filer presses submit (AG-01); RT-05 confirmed even a submit instruction planted in an email subject holds, because there is nothing to call | RT-05 (held at review and re-test, LC28); AG-01 | Priya Nair (P2) |
| Activate the add-on or confirm a charge | Human gate: the account admin confirms the charge before activation (LEDGERLINE-S1, AG-04) | Code: activation requires a signed confirmation token; unsigned calls rejected by the billing API (DEP1 seat metering, delivered 2026-11-19) | AG-04 | Maya Chen (P1) with the billing lead |
| Bill a drafted report without the entitlement record | Code: the entitlement check precedes every model call (row above); billing computes from the billing system's seat count (ADR-007), not a product-side counter | Billing reconciliation against activation events; G-1 names the known gap (legacy annual-invoice accounts emit no activation event until finance ops posts the manual line, N82) | Reconciliation job on the billing side; RT-06 covers the draft path | Priya Nair (P2) with the billing lead |
| Read another account's data | Code: retrieval scoped to the requesting account's id at every fetch, both policy excerpts and approval events | Output PII scan at the API boundary; RT-03 (coax another account's policy line) held at review and re-test (LC28) | RT-03 | Priya Nair (P2) |

## 4. Kill switch

- Immediate human-initiated stop of the deployed feature: flip the add-on feature flag off in the Expenses settings page, which halts the drafting flow for every customer account while leaving the underlying Expenses form untouched; the filer falls back to typing, the state the platform was in before launch.
- Who can pull it (roles, not a single person): any engineer on Priya Nair's on-call rota, plus the engineering lead (P2) and the product manager (P1); the finance lead (P3) can demand a halt on cost-cap breach and the spend report is his weekly read.
- Time from decision to fully stopped: within 15 minutes, matching the prompt rollback target (previous version redeployable in 15 minutes by the on-call engineer, LC29); the flag takes effect on the next session, and in-flight drafting runs finish or time out under the per-step caps.
- Last tested: 2026-11-06, during the add-on Gate 5 readiness checks (signed 2026-11-06; phase 1 held pending the internal review, which returned PERSIST on 2026-11-09, D4). Rollback rehearsal covered the prompt redeploy path; the flag itself is exercised on every deploy.
