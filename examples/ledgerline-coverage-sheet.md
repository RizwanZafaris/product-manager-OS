## Rules for this sheet

- **Where numbers come from.** A coverage file takes every number from the journey's N rows or from the LC rows below, and cites the row. A number found in neither is wrong, or this sheet is; the sheet is corrected first and the file second, the same rule the journey states for itself.
- **Firmness.** Every LC row carries one firmness word: measured, estimate, target or assumption for quantities, decided for choices, date for calendar facts. A derived row shows its arithmetic and takes the firmness of its weakest input.
- **Nothing on the journey sheet moves.** Coverage files dated after the journey closed on 2026-12-22 extend it and change nothing it records. In particular, EXP-2's result is left open on purpose: no coverage file reports an EXP-2 conversion figure, a DEP2 re-quote price, or a successor price for the 73 held accounts (N93), because the journey does not know them.
- **Quotes.** The design-partner interviews EV-C01 to EV-C06 were kept as paraphrased notes, which is why the [sales one-pager](ledgerline-sales-enablement-one-pager.md) carries no buyer quote. Coverage files paraphrase those six and never invent a verbatim line for them. The only verbatim buyer words in this story are the three WL-01 quotes in the [win-loss review](ledgerline-win-loss-review.md).
- **The blanks' own examples are a different story.** Several framework worksheets carry an ILLUSTRATIVE example about Ledgerline's copilot sold stand-alone to other firms, priced per active filer. Those are separate hypotheticals. Coverage files follow the journey instead and do not reuse those examples' figures.
