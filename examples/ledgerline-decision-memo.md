## 1. The decision

- **Question:** Should Ledgerline accept the model vendor's standard subprocessor terms in the customer DPA, with 30-day prompt retention and the existing no-training clause?
- **Door type:** One-way, because the door score is 10: undo 2 + cost to reverse 1 + time to learn 2 + who bears it 2 + forecloses 2 + blast radius 1 = 10. Reversing it would require every customer DPA to be re-papered.
- **Who decides:** Isabel Ferreira, chief product officer and pricing owner, because D5 is the decision blocking the Enterprise pipeline and the decision record names her as decider with the legal lead.
- **By when, and why then:** 2026-12-03, because the two Enterprise prospects blocked on the DPA, Halvard Marine and Ostrander Group, remain waiting. Each is estimated at about $2,400 MRR at the current per-seat price, and both quotes slip past year end if the team waits.
- **The default if nobody decides:** Wait for bespoke terms. Both Enterprise quotes slip past year end. This is option A.

## 2. Situation and complication

**Situation:** The customer DPA does not name the model vendor as a subprocessor. Risk R4 is open, and the customer DPA subprocessor addendum, DEP3, was needed by 2026-11-06 but was delivered late on 2026-12-03. The blocked Enterprise prospects are Halvard Marine and Ostrander Group, both in segment S-6, Enterprise accounts outside EXP-1.

**Complication:** The decision is now on the critical path for two Enterprise deals. The legal lead's preferred path is bespoke zero-retention terms, but that option is estimated at 6 to 8 weeks of legal time. Waiting for that path leaves both quotes blocked past year end.

**Resolution in one sentence:** Accept the model vendor's standard subprocessor terms in the customer DPA, with 30-day prompt retention and the existing no-training clause.

## 4. Recommendation

- **Option:** B, because it resolves the customer DPA blocker by the needed-by date and records the accepted terms precisely: 30-day prompt retention and the existing no-training clause. It gives the two Enterprise prospects a decided DPA position instead of carrying R4 as an unresolved blocker.
- **What it gives up:** It gives up the legal lead's preferred bespoke zero-retention terms and the time to negotiate them. It also accepts the model vendor's standard retention position rather than waiting for a bespoke amendment.
- **Conditions:** The legal lead confirms that the customer DPA carries the model vendor as a subprocessor, the 30-day prompt retention term and the existing no-training clause on 2026-12-03. The legal lead owns that confirmation. DEP3 is marked delivered on 2026-12-03.
- **Reversal trigger:** Reopen the decision if the accepted standard terms no longer contain the 30-day prompt retention and existing no-training clause, or if the customer DPA cannot carry those terms. The legal lead owns the review.
