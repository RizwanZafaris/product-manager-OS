## 1. Conventions and entities

- **Id format:** M-[three digits]; ids are never reused. A changed definition gets a new id or a version suffix; the alternative, silently redefining M-007 in place with no log entry, is what section 6 exists to prevent.
- **Time grain and timezone:** weekly, UTC, weeks starting Monday, except where a row states monthly or a point-in-time snapshot
- **Rounding and display:** percentages to one decimal place in review tables; dollars to the nearest dollar in review tables and to the cent in billing exports
- **Metric owner versus tile or KR owner:** the Owner column below names who defines and maintains this row. The dashboard spec and OKR sheet separately name who is accountable for the number where it is displayed, and the two roles differ on several rows: M-002 (dashboard T-3 tile owner: Kwame Boateng; OKR KR1 owner: Maya Chen), M-006 (dashboard T-2 tile owner and the dashboard's own section 5 alert-agreement text, both: Kwame Boateng; OKR KR2 owner: Priya Nair), M-007 (dashboard T-7 tile owner, named "the metric's owner" in the dashboard's section 3: Maya Chen), M-011 (dashboard T-11 tile owner: Daniel Okafor; OKR KR4 owner: Maya Chen). Where two sibling documents agree with each other, that is the stronger signal: M-006 below is set to Kwame Boateng because the dashboard's own tile-owner column and its alert-agreement text agree on him, even though the OKR sheet's KR2 owner is Priya Nair. M-007 has only one sibling value to go on, the dashboard's tile owner, so it is set to Maya Chen. M-002 and M-011 keep the metric-defining owner set below, Maya Chen and Daniel Okafor: their two sibling values point at different people (M-002: Kwame Boateng on the tile against Maya Chen on the KR; M-011: Daniel Okafor on the tile against Maya Chen on the KR), so there is no agreement to defer to, and this note is the honest record of the conflict, not a resolution of it.
