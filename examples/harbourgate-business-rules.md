## 4. Change control

- **Who may change a rule:** the named business owner in column 5 of section 1, never the product or engineering team acting alone. BR-001 and BR-005 sit with Saoirse Whelan, Fraud and Risk Lead; BR-002, BR-003, BR-006 and BR-009 sit with Priya Raman, Head of Finance Operations; BR-004 sits with Lena Baptiste, Head of Store Operations; BR-007 sits with Hamid Qureshi, Information Security Lead, since F4, the single-approver flag-change finding, is a security control rather than a business policy; Tomasz Wierzbicki, Engineering Lead, enforces the flag-change mechanics but does not own the rule.
- **How a change lands:** the business owner raises the change with Ife Adeyemi, Product Manager and register owner. A change that only tightens a threshold or retires a rule for lack of a flag to govern is recorded here and communicated to engineering; a change that alters what the product must enforce (a new threshold, a new trigger, a new exception) routes back through Gate 2 for re-signing before it ships, per D-018's precedent (Gate 2 attempt 3, 2026-05-28), when AC-3 was re-signed to add a per-cohort tolerance of baseline + 0.5 percentage points (N16), not to revise the N13 to N15 baselines themselves. The change reaches production through the same per-provider flag discipline BR-007 itself requires, where the rule touches routing.
- **Review cadence:** reviewed at every stage gate from Gate 2 onward, and additionally whenever a cohort in the migration cutover plan reaches 100% of a provider's traffic, since that is the point at which a legacy-path rule (BR-002, BR-007) changes what it governs. The full register was last reviewed at Gate 6, 2026-10-14, alongside D-024, which the sunset plan of 2026-10-16 executes and on which the refund tail (BR-002, BR-009) depends.

## Exit gate (feeds Gate 2: requirements signed off)

- [x] Every rule is atomic: one trigger, one outcome. The exception this register allows is a compound WHEN/THEN that shares one owner and one source and cannot be split without losing that traceability. BR-005's two triggers, an order over £250 or any fraud flag, both route to the one step-up requirement under Fraud policy v6, held by Saoirse Whelan; BR-001's two outcomes (offer a retry, never automatically re-submit the same card) and BR-004's two outcomes (show "pay at the till", hold the basket 30 minutes) are one behaviour under one source and one owner each, not two rules bundled together. BR-008, the retired 2019 cascade, read WHEN Kestrel declines THEN retry through Marlowe; the retirement note traces the defect to that retry running with no idempotency key, not to a second clause in the rule's own wording.
