## 1. Charter

**Program type:** customer advisory board

**The decision this program informs:** the recurring decision is the add-on's pricing and packaging document, section 1, each time it is reopened, and each add-on Gate 5 go call. Isabel Ferreira is the forum's decider; Hana Sato brings the board's input into that review. The board does not own a fixed slot on the pricing document's own calendar; its cadence follows the pricing document's own review cadence, section 6: every two quarters, and at each Gate 6 review of the add-on, plus an off-cycle review on a win-loss pattern naming the value metric, a competitor price move, or the EXP-1 experiment's own pre-declared kill rule. The 2026-12-02 session preceded the off-cycle review triggered on 2026-12-18 by EXP-1's kill rule and the win-loss pattern (pricing document section 6).

**What the program supplies that nothing else does:** a standing panel of S-5 finance decision-makers the pricing forum can put a live question to before a number ships, not after.

Session 1 on 2026-12-02 is the proof: six of eight members present said seat pricing penalises accounts with few filers, and five of eight asked for a per-report price instead (N78, EV-AB01), about two weeks before EXP-1's kill on 2026-12-18 (N61) and three weeks before the metrics review ratified the pivot on 2026-12-21 (D6). EXP-1's kill shows only that the seat price did not convert, not why; the seat-versus-filer mechanism came from the board on 2026-12-02 and, independently, from the win-loss batch, which found the same pattern, seats billed for non-filers or a price above the plan, in four of six interviews (N76, N77) that ran 2026-12-07 to 2026-12-16, after the board had already said it. No other existing channel puts a curated finance-buyer panel in the room ahead of a pricing decision; the design-partner pilot agreements (N41, N42) test the product, not the price, and the win-loss review only runs after a buying decision is reached or stalls.

## 2. Membership

- **Recruiting criteria:** decision-makers on Business-plan accounts in segment S-5, first-submission approval under 75% with five or more filers (N36), holding billing authority on the account (typically the account admin or the finance lead who sponsored the pilot). Disqualifies: accounts on the Starter or Enterprise plan (out of S-1's scope, N30, N31), and any account with an unresolved conflict such as a reseller relationship with Cinderwick. For any seat recruited after this charter's first revision, add: no new seat is drawn from an account currently inside an active pricing experiment's exposure or conversion window; the three original non-design-partner seats predate this rule, recruited before EXP-1's own exposure began, and are handled under the curation rule below instead.
- **Size and seats:** nine seats (N78). Six are held by the six design partners recruited for phase 1, Bramblewood Freight, Tessellate Consulting, Marlowe Field Services, Corrigan and Vale, Oakhurst Dental Partners and Wrenfield Labs (N41), chosen because they already carry firsthand product experience from the pilot rather than an opinion formed from a demo. The remaining three seats were recruited from S-5 outside the design-partner group before session 1, so the board is not entirely made up of accounts already holding the design partners' discounted 25% pilot terms (N42) when pricing questions come up; their identities are withheld under the board NDA and are not on the data sheet. Session 1 on 2026-12-02 had eight of nine members present (N78), so at least one of the nine seats, design-partner or otherwise, was absent that day; which seat is not on the data sheet.
- **Curation rules:** twelve-month terms from each seat's own start date, decided in this charter rather than sourced from the journey data sheet; the initial nine seats were staggered on recruitment so no more than three renew in the same cycle: three seats each ending 2027-05-10, 2027-08-10 and 2027-11-10, with twelve-month terms thereafter. A seat is retired at term end, on request, or after two consecutive sessions missed without notice; a design-partner seat converts to a standard S-5 seat if that account's pilot agreement lapses without conversion to a paid add-on account; no seat is held by an account with a direct competing vendor relationship. Because the three non-design-partner seats are drawn from S-5, which sits inside S-1, the population EXP-1 randomised, each member's EXP-1 arm (or non-exposure) is logged at recruitment and excluded from the analysis if a pricing discussion in a session falls inside that member's own exposure or conversion window; session 1 on 2026-12-02 fell inside EXP-1's exposure window (2026-11-23 to 2026-12-04) for any member exposed by that date, and that overlap is noted in EV-AB01.

## 3. Cadence and ownership

- **Meeting or cycle cadence:** the board follows the pricing and packaging document's own review cadence (section 6): every two quarters, and at each Gate 6 review of the add-on, plus an off-cycle session on a win-loss pattern naming the value metric, a competitor price move, or the EXP-1 experiment's own pre-declared kill rule. Session 1 on 2026-12-02 was held ahead of the 2026-12-21 Gate 6 review, in the same window the off-cycle trigger fired on 2026-12-18. The next program review is 2027-03-04.
- **Agenda owner:** Hana Sato; the agenda is built from the live state of the section 1 decision that cycle, not a product roadmap showcase.
- **Prep and follow-up:** a pre-read summarising the current state of the section 1 decision goes out one week before each session; Hana Sato closes the loop with members within ten business days on what their input changed, or, honestly, that it did not change the outcome that cycle. For session 1 on 2026-12-02, that deadline was 2026-12-16, two days before EXP-1's own kill result. Two follow-up touches were made: a loop-close note by 2026-12-16 telling members their concern was logged as EV-AB01 and that the pricing decision was still pending EXP-1's result; and a second note after the 2026-12-21 metrics review (D6) telling members, honestly, that EXP-1's kill (N61) confirmed only that the seat price did not convert, not why, and that the seat-versus-filer mechanism they raised was independently confirmed by the win-loss batch (N77), not by EXP-1 itself, and that their input had not overridden anything.

**The rejected option:** a fixed quarterly calendar slot for the board, independent of whether a pricing or packaging question was actually live, was argued at the charter session on 2026-11-10 and rejected. Hana Sato's reasoning, recorded here: a board that meets on a clock rather than a decision drifts into a dinner club with no live question in front of it, the exact failure mode this template's own guidance names. The board convenes on the pricing document's review cadence instead, so every session has a decision in front of it.

## 4. Terms

- **NDA:** required before the first session; standard mutual NDA, countersigned by the legal lead before the 2026-12-02 session, exact countersign date not on the data sheet.
- **Incentive, non-cash:** early visibility into the roadmap ahead of general release, a standing line to the product team outside a support ticket, and named recognition as an advisory board member, where the member consents to it. The design partners' 25% pilot discount (N42) is a separate commercial term of their pilot agreement, not a board-seat incentive, and the three non-design-partner seats hold no discount for their participation.
- **Data handling:** sessions are recorded only with member consent; notes are written as evidence notes per section 5 and stored in the shared evidence base with the same retention as other product evidence; access is limited to the product, customer success and pricing teams named in this file. Session notes and recordings hold member names and account data, which is personal data; no receipts or customer expense data enter these sessions. Retention and access follow the compliance impact assessment signed at Gate 2, owned by the legal lead (the PRD's launch criteria and the RICE sheet's mandate lane both route personal-data handling through that assessment); this program adds no data category the assessment does not already cover. This product's regulated overlay (the AI-and-financial-regulator module) is a separate question from data handling and does not apply here, since no financial or data regulator is named against this product in this journey.

## 5. Intake routing

- **Capture rule:** Hana Sato writes an evidence note within three business days of each session, decided in this charter. For session 1 on 2026-12-02 (a Wednesday), that deadline was 2026-12-07; EV-AB01 was captured on or before that date, exact date not on the data sheet.
- **Tag:** "Ledgerline Expenses Advisory Board", applied to every note the program produces, starting with EV-AB01 from the 2026-12-02 session.
- **Synthesis:** EV-AB01 joins the regular pass via [feedback-synthesis](../skills/feedback-synthesis/SKILL.md) with every other evidence note that cycle; the board's input gets no automatic priority over the win-loss batch, the support tickets, or any other evidence, and none was needed here: EXP-1's kill (N61) confirmed only that the seat price did not convert, not why, while the win-loss batch (N77) independently supplied the buyer evidence for the seat-versus-filer mechanism the board had already flagged; that mechanism, confirmed by N77 rather than by EXP-1 itself, shaped the usage-price design of the next bet, D7's $2.40-per-drafted-report re-offer (N89).

## 6. Exit criteria for the program itself

The program is reviewed at each program review date and retired when any of these holds:

- the pricing and packaging document's section 1 decision is retired (for example, if the add-on is sunset) or the pricing forum stops consulting the board before reopening a number
- attendance falls below six of nine seats for two consecutive sessions, decided in this charter (a floor, not a figure on the data sheet)
- two consecutive reviews of the section 1 decision cite no input from the program

At the 2027-03-04 review the board's next live question is whether D7's usage price clears its own 6.0% bar (N91, N92) and, since a per-drafted-report price carries no seat charge, whether it draws a new objection in the seat-count objection's place, such as an unpredictable monthly bill or the lack of a spending cap.
