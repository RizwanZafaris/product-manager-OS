## 1. Why partners, and which motion they extend

- The direct motion today, and what it cannot reach: direct sells well to companies of 250 to 1,000 employees with an in-house IT lead. It does not reach companies of 100 to 250, where IT is run by a managed service provider (MSP) and the buyer asks that provider what to buy.
- What partners supply that we cannot: MSPs own the buyer relationship and the implementation hours; audit-readiness advisory firms see the buying trigger (an upcoming external security audit) months before we do; the Corvane marketplace lets a buyer spend budget it has already committed to Corvane, which skips a new-vendor procurement.
- Evidence: of 23 lost deals in Q2 and Q3 2026, 9 were in the 100 to 250 band, and in 6 of those 9 the buyer bought the tool its MSP already supported (win-loss batch WL-K-04 to WL-K-12, in the product workspace, not tracked in this repository). In 11 of 31 won deals the buyer named an advisory firm as the reason they started looking. All counts ILLUSTRATIVE.

## 3. Partner economics: their margin and our net revenue

Direct, for comparison: $30,000 × (1 minus 0.12 average direct discount) = $26,400, minus $4,500 direct cost of sale = **$21,900 direct net**. All figures ILLUSTRATIVE, agreed with Gil Moreau on 2026-10-14.

| Type | List price per deal | Partner discount or fee | Partner margin per deal (incl. attached services) | Our net revenue per deal | Partner cost plus our own cost of sale, per deal | Our net after partner cost | Direct net, same deal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reseller, Gold tier | $30,000 | 25% discount | $7,500 at the $30,000 the MSPs have charged so far (their price, not ours), plus about $4,000 onboarding = $11,500 | $30,000 × 0.75 = $22,500 | $1,500 partner cost; $0 cost of sale | $21,000 | $21,900 |
| Referral, fee-paying firms | $30,000 | 10% of first-year contract | $2,640 fee | $26,400 contract minus $2,640 = $23,760 | $500 + $3,000 = $3,500 | $20,260 | $21,900 |
| Referral, Ostlund | $30,000 | none | $0; hours saved instead | $26,400 | $500 + $3,000 = $3,500 | $22,900 | $21,900 |
| Marketplace private offer | $30,000 | 3% marketplace fee | Corvane keeps $792 | $26,400 × 0.97 = $25,608 | $300 + $3,500 = $3,800 | $21,808 | $21,900 |
| Co-sell through the marketplace | $30,000 | 3% marketplace fee | Quota credit for Corvane's seller | $25,608 | $1,300 + $2,000 = $3,300 | $22,308 | $21,900 |

Three rows sit below direct net, and each earns its place differently:

- **Reseller, $900 below.** These deals would not happen direct: section 1's 6 of 9 lost deals in the 100 to 250 band went to the MSP's preferred tool. Silver (20%) and Registered (15%) resellers leave us more than Gold does, so Gold is the floor case.
- **Fee-paying referral, $1,640 below per deal, worth more per opportunity.** Referred opportunities closed at 48% in Q2 and Q3 against 29% direct. Per opportunity worked: 0.48 × $20,260 = $9,725 against 0.29 × $21,900 = $6,351. The fee is first-year only; renewals carry none.
- **Marketplace, $92 below.** Effectively level. Three of the last five marketplace buyers said they had no new-vendor budget this year, only committed Corvane spend, so those deals would not have happened direct.

We do not set any MSP's resale price. The $30,000 in the reseller row is what MSPs have chosen to charge so far, observed, and counsel (Ama Lindqvist, 2026-10-09) confirmed the plan contains no rule on partner pricing.

## 6. Deal registration and channel conflict

- Valid registration: named account, named contact and role, stated use case, and evidence of one meeting held, entered in the partner portal
- Approval time: 2 business days · Protection period: 90 days · Extension: once, for 45 days, with recorded activity in the last 30
- Direct seller compensation on a partner-sourced deal in their territory: full quota credit and half commission, so a direct seller loses little by approving a registration

| Situation | Rule | Who decides | Time to decide |
|---|---|---|---|
| A partner registers an account our direct team is already working | Direct keeps it if an open opportunity shows activity in the last 30 days; the partner is offered influence credit | Tomas Reid with the regional sales manager | 2 business days |
| Two partners register the same opportunity | The first approved valid registration wins; the second earns influence credit only through a qualifying action | Tomas Reid | 2 business days |
| An existing customer renews or expands | The reseller of record keeps renewals and expansions; direct customers stay direct unless the customer asks in writing to move | Hana Okafor | 5 business days |
| Our direct team is asked to quote against a registered partner deal | Direct does not quote and refers the buyer to the registered partner; any exception is approved and recorded by Hana Okafor | Hana Okafor | 1 business day |
| A protection period lapses with no recorded activity | The registration expires and the account is open | Portal rule, reported weekly to Tomas Reid | Automatic |

## 8. Metrics, with the definitions written out

- **Partner-sourced pipeline:** the value of qualified opportunities, in the quarter, for which an approved deal registration from a partner existed before any open Kiteline opportunity for that account. Credited to the registering partner only.
- **Partner-influenced pipeline:** the value of qualified opportunities, in the quarter, that a partner did not source but where a partner performed a recorded qualifying action within 120 days before close: a joint customer meeting, a technical validation in the customer's tenant, or a customer reference call. Reported separately; never summed with sourced.
- **Active partner:** a partner with at least one approved registration in the trailing two quarters.

## 9. How this plan fails

| Failure | The tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Partner count as the goal | Signed partners rose from 11 to 19 in two quarters while active partners stayed at 7 | Report active against signed every quarter, as section 8 now does; no new signings until 3 of the 12 inactive partners register a deal |
| Influenced pipeline added to sourced | The Q2 board slide showed partner pipeline of $940,000, which was sourced and influenced added together | The influenced line carries no target and appears only in its own column |
| A margin that does not pay for the effort | Four MSPs registered deals in Q2 that aged out with no activity | Rebuilt section 3 from two MSPs' own figures; the onboarding services line came from them |
| Direct sellers fighting partners | Two registrations rejected in Q2 for accounts with no open opportunity | Half commission plus full quota credit, and the 2-day approval time owned by Tomas Reid rather than the regional manager |
