## 3. Exception handling

| Rule ID | Exception | Who may grant it | Recorded where |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-014 | None. The block lifts only when the questionnaire is reconfirmed, which is the client's action, not a grant | Not grantable | The reconfirmation itself is the record, on the client's suitability record |
| BR-015 | None. Routing is recorded for every order; there is no path that skips the comparison | Not grantable | The per-order routing record |
| BR-017 | A mandated concentrated position in the client's written instructions | Farah Siddiqui, Best-Interest Compliance Officer | The client's mandate reference, reviewed at the annual suitability review |
| BR-018 | None for complex products. The execution-only carve-out below does not remove the appropriateness test | Not grantable | The appropriateness test result, on the client's record |
| BR-019 | None. The care obligation cannot be waived by disclosure alone | Not grantable | The recorded basis for each recommendation |
| BR-020, BR-021 | None. Pre-clearance and surveillance cover every covered employee | Not grantable | The pre-clearance record and the monthly surveillance log |

The execution-only carve-out is stated explicitly. Where a client, unprompted, instructs Ashford's execution-only account to buy or sell a specified instrument, Ashford takes no recommendation and no suitability duty attaches to that instruction, so BR-014's block does not apply and the instruction is executed. That carve-out is drawn at the line where advice stops and execution starts, and it does not cross it: BR-018's appropriateness test still applies where the instrument is complex, because MiFID II and UK COBS attach the appropriateness test to the transaction, not only to the recommendation. An execution-only order is not evidence that any Ashford recommendation was suitable, and the record says so.

## 4. Change control

- **Who may change a rule:** the named business owner of the rule, not the product team. Compliance holds change control for every rule in this register: Farah Siddiqui for BR-014, BR-017, BR-018, BR-019, BR-020 and BR-021, and Marcus Bell for BR-015 and BR-016. Where a rule carries a regulatory source, Compliance's signature is required before the change lands, and a rule cannot be changed on the release schedule alone.
- **How a change lands:** a written request from the rule's business owner stating the old statement, the new statement and the reason, reviewed by Compliance against the source of truth, then approved by Compliance and the business owner together. A rule change is a compliance change, not only a release: a retuned recommendation model, a new concentration limit or a changed venue-review cycle goes to Compliance for sign-off, then enters the build path as a new or superseding rule. The old row is retired into section 2, never edited in place.
- **Review cadence:** the register is re-confirmed against its sources quarterly, on the same cycle as the venue review in BR-016, at the close of each quarter.
