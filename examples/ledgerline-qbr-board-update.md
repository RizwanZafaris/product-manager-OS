## 1. Headline

The add-on product is working where measured, with 76% first-submission approval on drafted customer reports and 2.4 extraction errors per 100 drafted reports, but the per-seat price failed its pre-declared conversion rule and the launch KRs missed their goals. This quarter killed the per-seat price, held phase 3, and queued EXP-2, a usage re-offer at $2.40 per drafted report, with the same 6.0% activation bar. I ask the board to approve D11 (the board's FY2027 revenue plan decision, recorded as D11 in the coverage sheet): keep the add-on out of the FY2027 revenue plan until EXP-2 resolves on or before 2027-02-12.

## 4. Wins

- **Customer approval quality held:** 422 of 555 drafted reports were approved first time, 76% against the 66% customer baseline, with 2.4 extraction errors per 100 drafted reports, below the M-008 ceiling of 3.
- **The product earned a real paid base:** 79 active add-on accounts at 2026-12-18, comprising 73 paid accounts and 6 design partners.
- **The internal product cleared its adoption kill line:** 281 of 740 eligible reports were drafted, 38%, above the 30% kill line, and Gate 6 returned PERSIST (N25).
- **The failed price produced a clean decision:** EXP-1 exposed 2,748 accounts, with 33 of 1,371 control accounts paying, 40 of 1,377 anchored accounts paying, and 73 paid overall. Both arms were under the 4.0% kill line, so the EXP-1 rule killed the per-seat price on 2026-12-18, ratified as D6 on 2026-12-21.
- **The next bet is constrained rather than optimistic:** D7 keeps the 6.0% activation bar for EXP-2, with the usage meter needed by 2027-01-08 before planned exposure from 2027-01-11.
