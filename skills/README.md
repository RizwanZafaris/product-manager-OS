# Skills: the procedures

A skill here is a procedure a model executes, written as a file you can read. Not a plugin, not a hosted prompt, not a wrapper: one `SKILL.md` per skill, with exactly two frontmatter fields (`name` and a `description` that contains an explicit "Use when" clause), a list of the repo files the procedure drives, and the steps in order. Skills sit above [templates/](../templates/README.md), which they cite by path, and beside the role identities in [agents/](../agents/README.md): a template is the blank, a skill is the way you fill it, and an agent is who is holding the pen.

Nothing in a skill triggers itself. Triggering lives outside the skill, in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), so that the procedure and the routing can be reviewed separately.

## The nine skills

| Skill | What it does | Use when | Entry point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conductor | Runs the six-stage loop as an interview: one question at a time from the stage bank, weak answers cross-examined under a capped challenge grammar, every accepted answer landed in STATE.md and the target template, the gate rendered on evidence | A user says "start" on a new product, "resume" or "where are we" on an existing one, or wants to be walked through a stage rather than handed a blank | [conductor/SKILL.md](conductor/SKILL.md) |
| Product analyst | A staged research pass run by one person with one model: decompose, three-lens search, one evidence note per source with a verbatim quote, named tensions, committed positions with confidence labels, one adversarial pass before handoff | DISCOVER needs cited evidence behind a template, OPERATE needs metric evidence for Gate 6, or any claim about to enter a document would not survive "says who" | [product-analyst/SKILL.md](product-analyst/SKILL.md) |
| AI PRD | Restructures a PRD around the fact that the implementer is a model: acceptance criteria become eval sets, non-determinism becomes an explicit requirement, guardrails become features with owners | Specifying any feature where an LLM, agent, or model makes or drafts a decision, or reviewing a PRD that says "the AI should" anywhere | [ai-prd/SKILL.md](ai-prd/SKILL.md) |
| Roadmap builder | Turns a backlog and a strategy statement into a scored, sequenced roadmap with launch gates, dependency and risk columns, a regulatory calendar overlay, and the stakeholder conversations that follow scoring | Quarterly or annual planning from a raw pile of asks, or re-planning after a strategy shift, a funding change, or a new mandate | [roadmap-builder/SKILL.md](roadmap-builder/SKILL.md) |
| Program premortem | Checks a plan against the twelve ways program managers actually fail, returning which modes are already present, the evidence for each, and the smallest intervention that changes the outcome | Before kickoff of anything with three or more teams or an external dependency, before a cutover, or before Gate 3 as the risk half of "architecture and risks reviewed" | [program-premortem/SKILL.md](program-premortem/SKILL.md) |
| Reg gap check | Runs the compliance review in advance: a severity-ranked gap table with owners and the evidence that would close each gap, with every verified citation routed through the regulated module | A spec or roadmap item touches money movement, cards, wallets, lending, crypto, or customer data and you want the challenge before the regulator makes it | [reg-gap-check/SKILL.md](reg-gap-check/SKILL.md) |
| Feedback synthesis | Turns raw feedback into themes that carry their own evidence: source counts, contradictions kept rather than smoothed, and a so-what, landed as rows in the discovery templates | Interview transcripts, support tickets, sales notes, app reviews, or survey free text exist and the synthesis does not | [feedback-synthesis/SKILL.md](feedback-synthesis/SKILL.md) |
| Product review | Runs the weekly review as a truth-seeking WIP walk: a 48-hour pre-read, 20 minutes per team across pre-build, in-progress, and post-build work, decisions landed in the decision log the same day | An org has two or more teams shipping, or its reviews have decayed into demos, status, and theater | [product-review/SKILL.md](product-review/SKILL.md) |
| Escalation | Turns a stuck decision into a one-page brief (Situation, Impact, Urgency, Options, Recommendation, Ask) and routes it up a named ladder with SLAs, with the outcome recorded either way | A decision missed its needed-by date, two teams deadlock, a risk owner will not act, or a forum keeps noting things without deciding any of them | [escalation/SKILL.md](escalation/SKILL.md) |

The Conductor is the one skill with a directory of its own beyond the SKILL.md: its six question banks and their format live in [conductor/questions/](conductor/questions/README.md), one bank per stage, each question carrying an evidence class, a cross-examination trigger, and the template field the answer lands in. The normative protocol behind the skill is [os/CONDUCTOR.md](../os/CONDUCTOR.md), and where the two disagree, that file wins.

One more skill ships outside this directory. The tutor at [learn/skills/tutor/SKILL.md](../learn/skills/tutor/SKILL.md) teaches the OS on fictional products and reuses the Conductor's question banks read-only. It lives under `learn/` because that whole layer is deletable without touching the OS.

## How these load

**In an agent CLI.** Claude Code reads [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), Codex and other agent runtimes read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), and both map a request to a skill through the router table in those files. The runtime then follows the named `SKILL.md` exactly, reading the template and knowledge files the skill names as it goes. You do not invoke a skill by name; you ask for the artifact or say the trigger word, and the router picks.

**In a chat model with no file access.** Paste [system/BOOT-PROMPT.md](../system/BOOT-PROMPT.md) first, which installs the loop, the gate discipline, and the manifest of paths so the model asks for files by exact path rather than inventing one. Then paste the contents of the `SKILL.md` you want, and the templates it names when it asks for them. The procedure is identical either way, because the procedure is prose. What you lose without file access is the reading; what you keep is the method.
